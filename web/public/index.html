Academic Methodology & Formulations

1. Remote Sensing Radiometry

Active fire detection algorithms from the VIIRS (Visible Infrared Imaging Radiometer Suite) on the Suomi NPP satellite observe emitted radiance at 375m nominal resolution using mid-wave infrared (I4 channel, λ ≈ 3.74 μm) and thermal infrared (I5 channel, λ ≈ 11.45 μm). At sub-pixel scales, Planck's radiation law dictates that high-temperature combustion anomalies emit intense radiance in the mid-wave IR, enabling sensitive detection of sub-pixel fire fronts.

2. Vapor Pressure Deficit (Tetens Formula)

Vapor Pressure Deficit (VPD in kPa) reflects the atmospheric drying capacity driving dead fine fuel desiccation:

e_s(T) = 0.61078 * exp((17.27 * T) / (T + 237.3)) [kPa]
VPD = e_s(T) * (1 - RH / 100) [kPa]

3. Spatial Block Cross-Validation Design

Standard random K-Fold cross-validation results in severe spatial autocorrelation leakage (Tobler's First Law of Geography). Our pipeline divides the Indian subcontinent into spatial coordinate blocks, training on distant zones and testing exclusively on held-out geographic tiles to measure genuine spatial generalizability.
